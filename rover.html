The concept

Large foam wheels double as buoyant floats, with an integrated paddle design for water movement that minimizes turbulence in the air.

Early sketches

Concept sketches from the early design phase, including how the paddle wheels would move the rover through water.

Design semester

Spring 2026

Airframe components, structural chassis bays, dynamic seals and connection architecture were designed in PTC CREO and SolidWorks.

SolidWorks Flow Simulation was used to compare wheel profiles for drag against buoyancy.

Innovation Expo

Top New Project

The first frame iteration was printed and presented at the Fall 2026 Aggies Create Innovation Expo, where it won Top New Project.

Build semester

In progress

The design is done and the team is building toward an end-of-semester prototype. Current work includes a redesigned front frame half carrying DC and brushless motors.
