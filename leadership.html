PBE recruitment

Recruitment chair, January 2026 to now

As recruitment chair I organized 10 recruitment events, from interviews and brotherhood events to career-focused informationals, and built applicant tracking databases in Excel to process 150+ engineering candidates.

I managed the $2,000 recruitment budget across those events, cut costs about 10% through vendor relationships and bulk buying, and recouped $100 through a profit-share event. The Fall 2026 week ran from informationals and a career workshop to socials at the park and around town: staffing the table, running the grill, and showing prospective members what the brotherhood is really like.
