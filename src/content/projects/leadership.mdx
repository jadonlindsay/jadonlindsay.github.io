## The Goal

Run the chapter's full recruitment cycle, from first contact to final cuts, and bring in a strong new class while presenting PBE as the professional engineering organization it is.

## Setting the Tone: Professional From the First Touch

I wanted every point of contact to feel professional: emails, social media, the recruitment flyer, and the application itself. I designed the recruitment flyer and rebuilt and polished the application to reflect that, so applicants saw a serious, organized org from the first form they filled out.

<Gallery section="tone" />

## Planning Ahead

Recruitment started on the first day of school, so the work happened over the summer. I planned and organized 10 events, booking venues and lining up logistics before the semester began. The public lineup: two informationals, a pickleball social, a park grill-out, a career workshop, and a Blaze Pizza social, followed by interviews.

## Data-Driven Cuts

I built an Excel applicant database to process 150+ candidates, tracking application answers, majors, interests, class level, resumes, and event attendance. That gave the chapter a consistent, organized view of every applicant when it came time to make cut decisions.

## Stretching the Budget

I managed a $2,000 recruitment budget across all 10 events and came in about 10% under cost:

- **Venue:** booked ahead with Four Downs, which offered a discount for booking on a specific day, so I scheduled around it.
- **Food:** bought groceries in bulk at Costco for our park grill-out instead of catering.
- **Revenue:** set up the Blaze Pizza social as a profit-share night, which brought $100 back to the chapter while doubling as a recruitment event.

<Gallery section="budget" small />

## Keeping Members Engaged

Recruitment depends on current members showing up and actually connecting with applicants. A big part of the role was making sure actives attended events and engaged with potential new members, not just stood on the sidelines.

## Result

**21 new members** joined from 150+ applicants. The new class has been well received by current members, and the new members say they're happy to be in.
