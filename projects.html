Manpower tool

Project management intern, Dynamic Systems, summer 2026

Months of payroll timesheets, split by trade, hour type and employee, had to be consolidated by hand across dozens of sheets. I built a VBA and Python script that reads every timesheet tab, identifies each trade by job code, consolidates hours across entries, filters out weeks under 12 hours, and outputs a Weekly Overview table plus a 9-chart dashboard.

A 60-minute manual task now takes seconds, giving managers faster insight for budgeting and staffing.

Time
60 minutes to seconds
Output
Weekly Overview and a 9-chart dashboard
Tools
VBA, Python, Excel

Kinetic toy

MEEN 210, Geometric Design and Modeling

A functional kinetic toy built around a gear-driven Chebyshev lambda linkage, designed with a team. First built in 2D: SolidWorks models and laser-cut acrylic pieces, pinned, glued and painted.

Then rebuilt in 3D with more than ten custom printed gears, applying a 0.15 mm tolerance offset across pin joints and gear profiles to allow for printer shrinkage and laser kerf, and proven over three design iterations before the class Toy Fair.

Mechanism
Chebyshev lambda linkage
Tolerance
0.15 mm offset
Iterations
3, with 10+ printed gears
Tools
SolidWorks, laser cutting, 3D printing, GD&T
