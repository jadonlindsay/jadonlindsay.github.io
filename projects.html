Manpower Automation Tool

Project Management Intern · Summer 2026

Months of payroll timesheets — separated by trade, hour type, and employee — had to be consolidated by hand across dozens of sheets. I built a VBA and Python script that reads every timesheet tab, identifies each trade by job code, consolidates hours across entries, filters out sub-12-hour weeks, and outputs a Weekly Overview table plus a 9-chart dashboard.

A 60-minute manual task now takes seconds, giving managers faster insight for budgeting and staffing decisions.

60 min → seconds
Output

Tools
VBA, Python, Excel

04 / 04 · MEEN 210

2D & 3D Kinetic Toy

Geometric Design and Modeling

A functional kinetic toy built around a gear-driven Chebyshev lambda linkage, designed with a team. First built in 2D — SolidWorks models, laser-cut acrylic pieces, pinned and glued, then painted.

Then reimagined in 3D with 10+ custom 3D-printed gears, applying a 0.15 mm tolerance offset across pin-joints and gear profiles to account for printer shrinkage and laser kerf, and validated across 3 design iterations before presenting at the class Toy Fair.

Mechanism
Chebyshev lambda linkage
Tolerance
0.15 mm offset
Iterations
3, with 10+ printed gears
Tools
SolidWorks, laser cutting, 3D printing, GD&T
